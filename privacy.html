Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

TinyBar is a menu bar utility for macOS by Ihor Yanda. In short: your data never leaves your Mac.

No data collection

TinyBar does not collect, store, share, or sell personal data. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party code, and it never connects to the internet.

What stays on your Mac

  • Your settings (language, icon style, hiding options) and whether icons are hidden are saved only in the app's sandboxed storage on your Mac. Deleting TinyBar deletes them.
  • To show the drop zone, TinyBar notices when you hold ⌘ with the pointer in the menu bar. These events are used on the spot and never recorded.
  • TinyBar only resizes its own menu bar items. It does not read other apps' icons, windows, or content.

System features

"Launch at login" uses the standard macOS login items, and "Prevent Mac from sleeping" uses a standard macOS power setting. Neither shares any data.

Apple

Apple handles downloads, purchases, and updates through the App Store and TestFlight under its own privacy policy. If you send feedback or crash reports through TestFlight, Apple shares them with the developer.

Children

TinyBar collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be included in the app and published online with a new effective date.
